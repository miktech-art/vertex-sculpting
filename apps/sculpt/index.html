Controls

Drag A to change attack time. Drag D to change decay time and sustain level. Drag S to change hold time and sustain level. Drag R to change release time. Parameters update the playing sound live.

Lower pitch maps to greater line thickness by default. Drag the visual to rotate it; scroll to zoom.

In Connections, connect sound parameters to visual properties. Select a line to adjust strength, response curve, inversion, and smoothing.
