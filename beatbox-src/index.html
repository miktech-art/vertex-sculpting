EVERY CORNER
IS A NOTE.

A black dot travels the outline of every form. Distance is time: one grid unit is one beat, so a form's perimeter is its loop length, and snapping to the 1/16 grid keeps every corner on the beat. Corners are notes and open faces are rests. Height is pitch: every corner plays the note at the height the dot passes it, with a plane for every octave, so lifting a face changes its notes. Colour is the instrument and brightness the pitch. Drop one form onto another to chain bars into a stack. The top view (numpad 7) shows every form around one centre. The 3D view pulls them apart so you can edit them separately.
